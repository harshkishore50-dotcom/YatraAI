The core system

Three labels. Every fact, every time.

Nothing enters an itinerary unlabeled. This is the rule the rest of the product is built around.

VER

Verified

Pulled from a cited, reliable source — official tourism boards, transit authorities, park services. The citation is always visible, never buried.

EST

Estimated

An AI inference from patterns or partial data — flagged clearly as a best guess. Confirm locally before relying on it for anything time-sensitive.

USR

Community

Submitted by other travelers. Useful color and tips, but never treated as fact — and never used for safety or accessibility guidance.
